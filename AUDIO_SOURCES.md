# Audio sources

Hankorab ambience loop (entry_frontend/entry_frontend/ambience_hankorab.js) is an offline mix of three real recordings, all CC0 1.0 (no attribution required, credited here anyway):

- Wind: "Looping Gentle Wind Ambience on an Open Desert Plain" by dhallcomposer, https://freesound.org/people/dhallcomposer/sounds/697217/
- Waves: "Calm Waves ambience" by priesjensen, https://freesound.org/people/priesjensen/sounds/520770/
- Crickets: "Quiet Night Atmosphere - Soft Crickets (Eagle Mountain, Utah)" by Goldenboy76, https://freesound.org/people/Goldenboy76/sounds/857163/

Mix: mono, 22.05 kHz, 24 kbps MP3, 36 s seamless loop. The menu track and the hotel and lake ambiences are generated in code from the page's own sine and noise synthesis.
